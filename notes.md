Moving body
The head will move as we wish, the rest of the body will move with the same spead, but the direction
will be determined, not by our input, but by the distance between the cell and the previous cell, if
the distance is larger than the size of a rectangleis because the previous cell made a turn, so the
state of that cell will be checking WHEN both cells align on x or y axis again, and once that
happens, this cell will keep on moving in the same direction.
The direction will be something like, x1-x2 , y1-y2) Which will give 0 and something, that something
being the direction.
